# PULSE executive demo script (3–5 minutes)

This follows the acceptance scenario in PRD §11.4. Start from a cold launch: force-stop the app first so the opening animation plays. To reset mid-demo, open **⋯ → Reset demo**. Airplane mode can stay on throughout.

---

## 0. Opening (15 s)

**Launch the app.** A blue heartbeat line sweeps across the screen, the PULSE logo appears, then it flies into the header as Overview fades in. Tap to skip it.

> "Everything you'll see runs from **synthetic sample data** bundled in the app. The people and business units are ours, but every number is invented and the app isn't connected to monday.com. The `DEMO DATA` badge stays on screen the whole time, and every figure is calculated from the same fixed dataset, dated 31 October 2026."

## 1. Overview: the health check (60 s)

**Screen:** Overview, *Executive overview*, period **Month** (October 2026).

- Point to the **Booked sales** card, which shows about **£75k**. Say: "This is the value of won deals, not recognised accounting revenue."
- Point to the **vs previous** pill and the **target bar** under it.
- Swipe the **KPI strip**: win rate, lead-to-win time, open pipeline, target attainment and stalled deals.
- Point to **Top performers**: Mamun (Lawyer), Irteza (HR) and Sunny (Cloud) are within about £1.5k of each other. Mamun's £7,900 JR win is the month's biggest deal.
- Point to **By unit**. Lawyer is the biggest unit this month, at about £22k or nearly a third of booked sales.
- Tap **Lawyer**. *Unit detail* shows its top products and who sold them.
- Tap **Back**.
- Scroll to **Booked sales vs target** and touch and hold the chart to scrub across the months.
- Tap **30D**, then **Month**, to show every card recalculating together.

> "Ten seconds in, we know whether we're on target and which unit is carrying the month. The next question is how quickly we're converting."

## 2. Conversion: where momentum is lost (60 s)

**Tap:** **Median lead-to-win** in the KPI strip, or the **Conversion bottleneck** card. This opens *Conversion analytics*.

- Scroll to the stage dwell list and read out the banner **"<Stage> is the slowest stage"**, including its median days and sample count.
- Point out that the screen says this is an observation, not a causal claim.
- For the guided version, open **⋯ → Presentation scenarios → Conversion bottleneck**: Lawyer **JR** deals are dwelling in Proposal.

> "Deals spend longest at this stage, and Judicial Review work is where it shows most. That's where to look first."

**Tap:** Back.

## 3. One deal, end to end (45 s)

**Tap:** the **Pipeline** tab, then any deal card. Each card shows its product and unit, e.g. "Visa · Lawyer". The value flies into the deal header.

- Walk down the **Lead journey**: lead received → first contact → opportunity → won. Note that each starting point is kept separate.
- Walk down the **Stage timeline**: dated milestones with time spent between them.
- Tap a stage to open its detail sheet, then tap the **owner** row.

> "Here's the whole path for one deal. Now, who owns it, and where does their time go?"

## 4. Sales vs operations (45 s)

**Tap:** Back until you reach Pipeline, then tap the **Overview** tab. Scroll to the **Sales vs operations** card and tap it. This opens *Workload*.

- Read the **operations share of logged time**.
- Tap **Operations** in the legend. The sheet lists the activities behind the figure, and records with no logged duration show **Not recorded**.
- Close the sheet and scroll to **Workload by rep**. Irteza (HR) carries the most operational follow-up.

> "This is logged activity time, not total hours worked. Some of our sellers carry a lot of onboarding and support after the sale."

## 5. Team: outcomes with context (45 s)

**Tap:** the **Team** tab, *Team performance*.

- Switch the ranking between **Booked sales**, **Target**, **Win rate** and **Cycle time**, and watch the rows reorder.
- Point to each rep's home-unit tag and the **"N closed"** sample sizes. Reps with fewer than 5 closed deals, like Shahriar on the new Global unit, are ranked after the larger samples.

> "There's no composite productivity score here, just descriptive views with their denominators visible."

## 6. The management question (20 s)

**Tap:** back to the **Overview** tab.

> "*Could a better sales-to-operations handoff free selling capacity and shorten conversion time?*"

---

### If something goes wrong

- Lost your place: **⋯ → Reset demo**, or pull down on Overview.
- Guided jumps: **⋯ → Presentation scenarios → Conversion bottleneck / Operational workload / Team comparison**.
- Light/dark: **⋯ → Dark theme** (light is the default).
